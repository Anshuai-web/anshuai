Privacy Policy

How AnshuAI Technologies Pvt. Ltd. safeguards enterprise confidential information, customer records, and candidate personal data under global sovereign data protection frameworks.

Effective Date: October 1, 2026 • Version 2.4 • Entity: AnshuAI Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

Executive Commitment to Sovereign Data Integrity

AnshuAI Technologies Pvt. Ltd. ("AnshuAI", "we", "us", or "our") operates as an enterprise digital transformation consultancy and Certified Technical Architect (CTA) advisory firm. We are committed to upholding the highest standards of data privacy, sovereignty, and security. We adhere strictly to the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India), the General Data Protection Regulation (EU/UK GDPR), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA).

Zero Client Model Training: AnshuAI never uses proprietary client code, instance data, or customer confidential records to train public commercial AI models. All sovereign AI implementations operate in air-gapped or dedicated enterprise-tenant boundaries.

2. Categories of Personal Data We Collect

We collect personal and professional data solely to provide enterprise architecture advisory, process client service requests, and evaluate candidate applications:

  • Business Contact & Inquiry Data: When requesting a CTA Value Assessment or contacting us, we collect your full name, work email address, telephone number, organization name, job title, and project specifications.
  • Candidate & Recruitment Data: When applying via our Careers portal, we collect your name, email, phone number, LinkedIn/GitHub portfolio links, architectural experience highlights, and any voluntarily submitted resume documentation.
  • Automated Technical Telemetry: When navigating our site, we record anonymized technical details such as your IP address (truncated for geolocation compliance), browser type, referring URL, time zone, and pages viewed for DDoS prevention and performance auditing.
  • Interactive Tools & Estimator State: Parameters entered into our interactive ROI Estimator are processed entirely client-side in your local browser session and are not stored in central databases unless you explicitly submit a booking inquiry.

4. Data Retention & Storage Limitations

We do not retain personal information longer than necessary to fulfill the operational purpose for which it was collected:

  • Advisory Inquiries: Retained for 12 months following last contact, after which records are permanently expunged or anonymized unless superseded by a formal Master Services Agreement (MSA).
  • Candidate Records: Retained for a maximum of 24 months to evaluate candidates for subsequent engineering sprint openings, after which files are deleted unless the candidate requests earlier erasure.
  • Security & Web Server Logs: Rotated and overwritten on a strict 30-day rolling cycle.

5. Cross-Border Transfers & Dual-Continent Architecture

AnshuAI operates an engineering Center of Excellence in Bengaluru, India, serving global customers across North America, EMEA, and APAC in partnership with our US parent group, Vellicor Technologies Ltd.

Any cross-border transfer of personal information is executed in strict adherence to:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) as approved by the European Commission.
  • Transfer mechanisms sanctioned under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023.
  • End-to-end transport layer encryption (TLS 1.3) and strict role-based access control (RBAC).

6. Your Statutory Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you possess statutory rights regarding your personal data under the DPDP Act 2023, GDPR, or CCPA:

Right to Access & Summary

Request confirmation of whether we process your data and obtain a summary of identities shared.

Right to Correction & Erasure

Rectify inaccurate personal data or request permanent deletion of obsolete records.

Right to Withdraw Consent

Withdraw previously granted consent for advisory communications at any time with immediate effect.

Right to Grievance Redressal

Lodge formal complaints directly with our statutory Grievance Officer before escalating to regulatory boards.

Zero Sale of Personal Data: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade personal data or candidate resumes to third parties under any circumstances.
