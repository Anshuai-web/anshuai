Cookie Policy

How AnshuAI Technologies Pvt. Ltd. utilizes essential cookies and local session storage to deliver secure, responsive enterprise experiences without invasive cross-site tracking.

Effective Date: October 1, 2026 • Version 2.4 • Entity: AnshuAI Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

Our Privacy-First Approach to Web Storage

AnshuAI respects your online privacy. We do not use third-party advertising tracking networks, invasive behavioral profiling pixels, or cross-site fingerprinting scripts. We utilize only strictly necessary cookies and local storage tokens required to maintain session security, render interactive tools, and record your consent preferences.

1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies are small text data files placed on your computer or mobile device by a web server when you visit an online portal. Local Storage is a modern web browser technology that permits web applications to store preferences locally on your machine with zero server-side transmission overhead.

2. Categories of Storage We Deploy

Category 1: Strictly Necessary / Essential Storage Always Active

Essential to the operation and security of the website. These tokens manage network routing, Content Security Policy enforcement, anti-DDoS integrity, honeypot spam protection, and storing your explicit cookie consent choice. Because the website cannot function properly without these, they cannot be disabled.

Category 2: Functional & Preference State User Controlled

Enables enhanced functionality and personalization, such as remembering your industry selection in our interactive ROI Estimator or your preferred view mode. They do not track you across other websites.

Category 3: Anonymized Performance & Telemetry Aggregated Only

Helps us understand how visitors interact with our pages, diagnose latency spikes, and optimize responsive layouts. All telemetry is aggregated and stripped of personally identifiable information (PII).

3. Specific Storage Tokens Inventory

Key Name Mechanism Category Lifespan Purpose
anshuai_cookie_consent localStorage Strictly Necessary 12 Months Stores your consent preference ('all' or 'essential') to avoid repeating the consent banner.
anshuai_calc_state sessionStorage Functional Session End Temporarily caches ROI calculator slider values during your browser session.

4. Managing Cookies in Your Web Browser

You can block, disable, or delete cookies at any time through your web browser configuration. Most modern browsers provide detailed instructions:

Google Chrome Apple Safari Mozilla Firefox Microsoft Edge

If you have questions regarding our use of cookies or local storage technologies, please contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@anshuai.in or refer to our comprehensive Privacy Policy.
